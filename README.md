# Hi, I'm Yi (Justice) Zhang

I'm a production ML/NLP engineer and a Master of Data Science in Computational Linguistics student at UBC. I spent more than two years building and operating multilingual LLM and NLP systems at BIGO, from data and supervised fine-tuning through evaluation, serving, rollout, and human-in-the-loop workflows.

My research interests include LLM engineering and agentic AI applications. I have co-authored work on function calling and multi-agent collaboration.

## Selected publications

- [mABC](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.232/) — multi-agent collaboration for root-cause analysis, Findings of EMNLP 2024.
